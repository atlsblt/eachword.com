Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

EachWord makes music apps for independent artists. This policy covers the website eachword.com and the EachWord apps. Questions go to b4echo@eachword.com.

This website

The site has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no analytics, and it sets no cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement for details. Fonts are served from eachword.com itself, so no third-party requests are made when a page opens.

Our apps

Rhythm Animator, Lyric Sync and Canvas Loop process audio, images and lyrics on your own computer. The files you open in them are not uploaded to us. If a future version needs an online service, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the app will tell you what is sent.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your email address and message only to reply and to follow up on what you asked about, such as beta access. We don't sell it or add you to a mailing list without asking. Ask us at any time to delete your messages.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing b4echo@eachword.com.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
