How Lyric Sync works

Lyrics that stay on the voice

On-screen words usually drift because they are timed to the whole mix. We time them to the singer.

  1. Hear the full mixDrums, bass and pads hide where a phrase really starts.
  2. Pull the voice outStem separation leaves only the vocal to listen to.
  3. Mark every phraseSpeech recognition finds the first syllable of each line on the stem.
  4. Lock it to the barMarkers snap to the 2-bar grid and the words light up on time, within a fifth of a second.

Rhythm Animator

Turns a still cover into a video that moves with the track. It reads the beat, lets you design the animation in the middle of the artwork and exports in 4K, ready to finish in your video editor.

  • Beat detection from the audio file
  • Animation designer with live preview
  • 4K export, rendered on your Mac

Lyric Sync

Takes a song and its lyrics and returns timed captions for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. It listens to the separated vocal instead of guessing from loudness, then checks the rendered clip again.

  • Vocal stem separation
  • Phrase timing checked against the bar grid
  • SRT and burned-in caption output

Canvas Loop

Builds the short vertical loop that streaming apps play behind a track, made from the cover's own scene: light that pulses, mist that drifts, a slow shimmer on water.

  • Seamless loop point
  • Motion taken from the artwork itself
  • Sized for Spotify Canvas
